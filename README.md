<h2>🆘 Troubleshooting Common Issues</h2>

<h3>❓ DarQ isn't applying dark mode to an app</h3>
<ul>
<li>Make sure the app is not excluded in your settings. Check the toggle next to that app.</li>
<li>Try restarting the target app after enabling dark mode for it.</li>
<li>If you use LSPosed mode, ensure the module is activated in the LSPosed manager and you have rebooted after activation.</li>
</ul>

<h3>❓ Shizuku mode isn't working</h3>
<ul>
<li>Ensure Shizuku is properly installed and running. Open the Shizuku app and tap "Start" to activate it via wireless debugging or ADB.</li>
<li>Re-open DarQ after Shizuku is active to re-establish the connection.</li>
<li>Check that your Android version is compatible with Shizuku (Android 11+ recommended for wireless debugging).</li>
</ul>

<h3>❓ Scheduled switching doesn't trigger</h3>
<ul>
<li>Verify the schedule is enabled in settings.</li>
<li>Ensure DarQ is not being killed in the background. Check your device's battery optimization settings and exclude DarQ from aggressive battery saving.</li>
<li>Try toggling the schedule off and on again to reset the timer.</li>
</ul>
